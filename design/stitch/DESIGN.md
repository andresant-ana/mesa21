## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, intimate, tabletop social experience. It avoids high-roller casino tropes, flashing neon skeuomorphism, and cold corporate fintech conventions. Instead, it embraces an indie game sensibility: tactile, organic, inviting, and uncluttered.

### Personality & Tone
- **Acolhedor (Welcoming):** Soft cream foundations, rounded contours, and calm pacing evoke casual card nights among close companions.
- **Lúdico & Discreto (Playful yet Restrained):** Micro-interactions have spring and bounce, while the layout retains clean negative space and deliberate restraint.
- **Despretensioso (Unpretentious):** Natural Brazilian Portuguese phrasing, unhurried animations, and soft organic framing replace hyper-stimulating casino indicators.

### Style Movements
The interface combines **Tactile Minimalism** with **Organic Playfulness**:
- **Table Felt Grounds:** The deep, velvety forest-teal green anchor sets the focal game zone, treating the table as an artisanal felt mat.
- **Paper & Card Tactility:** Elements mimic heavy matte cardstock through soft ambient shadows and layered depth rather than skeuomorphic glossy gradients.
- **Pill Geometry:** Extensive pill rounding (level 3) softens edges and eliminates industrial rigidity.

## Colors

The palette balances the grounding depth of traditional game felt with radiant, friendly warmth.

### Primary Role (Velvet Green Felt)
- `#1D3F36` serves as the primary brand color and the foundational game table surface.
- Surface shifts and elevated felt areas use `#234E43`, while active indicators and winning streaks leverage vibrant mint accents (`#34D399`).

### Secondary & Accent Roles
- **Coral/Peach (`#FF6B57`):** Primary interactive actions, hit triggers, and high-energy alerts. Warm, dynamic, and non-punitive.
- **Butter Yellow (`#FFE082`):** Stand actions, active dealer chips, and score badges. Brings nostalgic tabletop warmth.
- **Lavender Soft (`#A78BFA`) & Sky Mist (`#38BDF8`):** Player avatars, social sidebars, spectators, and ambient chat highlights.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm cream (`#FAF8F5`) for application frame and secondary wrappers; card surface neutral sits at `#F3EFEA`.
- **Text & Foreground:** Deep charcoal slate (`#1E232A`) for primary headings and card ranks; subdued body copy uses `#2D343F` and `#5A6578`.
- Contrast strictly complies with WCAG AA standards across all text and interactive triggers.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, expressive display labels and titling with **DM Sans** for relaxed, readable running body copy.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Rounded geometric terminals give game actions ("Pedir Carta", "Parar", "Dobrar") a friendly, tactile presence without feeling childish. Used for points, hand totals, player names, and prominent controls.
- **DM Sans:** Delivers balanced legibility for match chat, table settings, hand histories, and community rules.
- **Number Treatment:** Card ranks and score tally counters utilize tabular figures where possible to prevent layout jitter during rapid score increments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a contextual focal canvas (the felt arena) centered within a generous, soft cream envelope.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column layout with max container width constrained to `1440px`. The blackjack table occupies an organic central oval container flanked by floating modular docks (player profiles, social chat, and betting tray).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. The central table scales down smoothly; chat shifts to a slide-over pill drawer.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Reflows to a vertical layout with the dealer zone top, card play center, and player action bar anchored in a bottom sheet with `margin-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Generous internal padding prevents physical touch targets from overcrowding the card mats.
- Card overlaps use controlled negative margins (`-1.25rem` horizontal overlap) to simulate hands held casually in real life.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft tonal layering and warm, diffuse ambient drop shadows rather than hard structural borders.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Piso 0 (Base Room):** Warm off-white canvas (`#FAF8F5`). Flat, non-reflective.
2. **Piso 1 (A Mesa / Felt Table):** Inset felt surface (`#1D3F36`) with a subtle deep green inner ambient shade (`inset 0 4px 16px rgba(10, 24, 20, 0.45)`) that conveys a sunken, physical tabletop groove.
3. **Piso 2 (Cartas e Fichas / Cards & Chips):** Smooth cream cardstock (`#FFFFFF` to `#F3EFEA`) elevated by warm ambient tinting: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(29, 63, 54, 0.12), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(29, 63, 54, 0.08)`.
4. **Piso 3 (Controles Flutuantes / Floating Dock):** Action pads and modal dialogs lifted above the table using high diffusion: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(20, 35, 30, 0.18)`.

### Ambient Depth Rules
- Borders are kept minimal; when separation is required, use tone-on-tone 1px soft outlines (`rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.06)`).
- Never use harsh black box shadows. All shadows inherit a subtle green-teal or charcoal chromatic tint.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature (pill-shaped level 3).

### Curvature Tokens
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `1rem` (16px) minimum rounding.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for high-focal calls to action.
- **Cards & Dialogue Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `2rem` (32px) soft curves providing a friendly, paper-weight card feel.
- **The Table Felt (`rounded-xl`):** `3rem` (48px) up to complete stadium pill shapes to recreate an organic curved card arena.

Geometry remains smooth, tactile, and continuous, avoiding hard 90-degree corners anywhere in the interactive canvas.

## Components

### Botões (Buttons)
- **Botão Primário ("Pedir", "Nova Partida"):** Pill shape (`9999px`), background Coral Vivo (`#FF6B57`), text white, bold Plus Jakarta Sans. Smooth downward press micro-translation (`translateY(2px)`) on active state.
- **Botão Secundário ("Parar"):** Pill shape, background Butter Yellow (`#FFE082`), text Charcoal (`#1E232A`). Warm and unthreatening.
- **Botão de Mesa ("Dobrar", "Dividir"):** Translucent felt glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` over dark green felt), white text, soft hover outline.

### Cartas de Jogo (Game Cards)
- Form factor with `rounded-lg` (24px to 32px), off-white warm finish (`#FFFFFF` face with subtle `#F3EFEA` border edge).
- Clear rank display in top-left and inverted bottom-right corners.
- Red suits (Copas, Ouros) feature vibrant Coral/Terracotta rather than pure primary red. Black suits (Espadas, Paus) use Deep Charcoal (`#1E232A`).

### Fichas e Marcadores (Chips & Score Pills)
- Circular and pill badges with concentric soft inner rings.
- Scores ("21", "Estourou", "Mesa") sit within high-contrast pills floating centered above or beside player card spreads.

### Chat & Social Dock
- Compact bubble stream using warm off-white containers (`#FAF8F5`) resting outside the felt perimeter.
- Player avatars framed in colored candy rings (Lavender, Butter, Sky Blue).

### Campos de Entrada (Input Fields)
- Table name and bet amount inputs use fully rounded capsules (`1.5rem` / 24px) in Cream (`#F3EFEA`), with subtle deep charcoal text and zero-harsh outline borders. Focus produces a soft mint halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.35)`).

### Caixas de Seleção & Opções (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded circular and smooth square toggles with soft cream backgrounds and filled mint green (`#34D399`) active check states.
